No app. No web page. No QR code to photograph across a table. You claim a name, you fill in the fields you want people to find you by, and from then on anyone with a terminal can pull your card out of thin air.

## What an engraving is

Real ANSI art, in color, drawn into a box that sizes itself to your longest line and stays inside 76 columns so it never wraps in an 80-column terminal. It is rendered the moment it is asked for, so an edit you make now is what the next person sees.

A free card carries your name and up to five fields, whatever you like. Free stays free.

## The wares

- **Extended Card.** Fifty fields instead of five, and room for the long stuff: a full PGP fingerprint, a Monero address, an onion link. Long values get their own panel below the box instead of being cut off.
- **Seal Access.** Seals are the little engraved figures that sit in the corner of the box: a coffee cup, a sailboat, a skateboard, a pianist, a school of fish. Sixty-plus of them in the shop, and the pass unlocks the lot.
- **Skin passes.** A skin is the card's color palette, the whole box recolored. Thirty-plus skins across three tiers, bought independently, no ladder to climb.
- **Limited drops.** Seasonal engravings that appear for a window and then go back in the drawer. If you own one, it stays yours and stays on your card after it leaves the shop.

There is no subscription, there never will be, and what you buy is yours to use.

## Printed, if you want something to hand over

The same engraving, struck onto paper: 3.5 x 2 inches, 300 gsm uncoated, in sets of 50 or 100. Your card is re-rendered at print resolution rather than screenshotted, so the box edges stay crisp instead of going soft at the corners. Printed and shipped to US (only) addresses.

## Claim your name

Go to **[curltact.com](https://curltact.com)**, sign in with GitHub, GitLab or Steam, and pick a name. There is no password to invent. You are live in about a minute, and then your card is one command away from everybody you ever meet again.

```console
$ curl curltact.com/your-name
```
